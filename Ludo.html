Official Ludo Rules

1. Leaving the Base: You must roll a 6 to move a token out of your starting yard onto your colored starting square.

2. Bonus Rolls: Rolling a 6, capturing an opponent's token, or getting a token all the way into the center triangle grants you an immediate Bonus Roll!

3. Three Sixes Penalty: Rolling three 6s in a row forfeits your current turn and passes the dice to the next player.

4. Capturing Opponent Tokens: Landing on a square occupied by an opponent's token captures it and sends it back to their home yard!

5. Safe Zones (): All 4 colored starting squares and the 4 white starred squares are Safe Zones. Tokens on safe squares cannot be captured by anyone.

6. Winning the Game: Tokens must complete a full 51-step circuit around the board before entering their colored Home Stretch. You need an exact roll to enter the center finishing triangle. First player to get all 4 tokens home wins!
